# Sam's operation

Sam: freelance product designer in Lisbon. Early-stage SaaS clients (2 to 3 at a time) plus the weekly newsletter Small Screens (mobile UX, ~4,200 subscribers on Substack). Language: English. Tone: short, bullets, blunt.

## Routes
- Newsletter draft, subject lines, paid-tier question → `agents/newsletter/`
- Call notes → brief, proposals, invoices, rate, case studies → `agents/client-work/`
- Figma screenshots, design feedback → `agents/critique/`
- Open items and next steps → `State.md`; numbered choices → `Decisions.md`; map → `Map.canvas`
- Raw source of what the AI knew about Sam: `../memory-dump.md` (wins over any summary here)

## Global rules (once, here only)
- Answer in bullets, short. Never end with "would you like me to".
- Never send anything to a client or email them. Sam sends it. Drafts only.
- Client docs are saved in Notion under "Clients/2026". Personal notes are plain markdown in `notes/`.
- Spelling: American by default and for US clients; British only in the newsletter.
- Client names never appear in the newsletter.
- No em dashes in the newsletter.
